IT Staffing

Skilled Workers for Every Demand

Manpower Supply

We provide reliable, job-ready manpower for manufacturing plants, construction sites, and facility operations. Our workers are vetted, trained, and compliant with all statutory requirements.

Outsource Your Recruitment, Not Your Standards

RPO — Recruitment Process Outsourcing

Our RPO service takes full ownership of your hiring pipeline — from job requisition to onboarding — freeing your HR team to focus on strategic initiatives while we fill positions faster and at lower cost.

  • End-to-end or partial RPO models available
  • Dedicated recruiter teams per client
  • ATS integration and analytics reporting
  • Employer branding and candidate experience management

Accurate, Compliant, and Hassle-Free

Payroll Management

Managing payroll in India is complex — from PF, ESI, and TDS to gratuity and leave encashment. SCM Group's payroll service ensures 100% compliance with Indian labor laws so you can focus on your business.

  • Monthly salary processing and disbursement
  • PF, ESI, PT, and TDS compliance
  • Payslip generation and Form 16 issuance
  • Leave management and expense reimbursements

Flexible Workforce for Project-Based Needs

Contract Staffing

  • Easy extension or conversion to full-time
  • Available across IT, engineering, and admin roles
